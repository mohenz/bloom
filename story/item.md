Team Members: (The PM is yl angry... dinner is ruined today. I should stay quiet.)

Yelim: (Wiping tears) "Really... you're too much! You yelled yl scarily in front of everyone... hmph..." (Complaining while hugging Brian)

The two exchange secret smiles as the car exits the parking lot.
