# Detailed Storyline (Single Volume)

This document is the **sole detailed storyline reference document** for this work.
It is organized around the development sequence of emotions, information, and conflicts that the reader will actually follow,
and includes arrangements that deliberately deviate from the timeline (information delay, reversals).
Scenes and dialogue will be added in later stages.

---

## Prologue ─ The Attitude of Responsibility
- XPlan live service failure occurs
- User distrust and public pressure grow larger than the technical cause
- Yeonjin proposes setting **standards for trust recovery and fairness** before apologizing
- Donggun makes the decision to step forward as the technical lead
- The crisis is calmed, but a bond of trust is formed between the two

---

## Act 1 ─ Time Spent Together (Building Trust)

### 1. Project Planning
- New game project kickoff
- Donggun prioritizes stability and structure
- Yeonjin prioritizes user experience and long-term trust
- Although the perspectives are different, it becomes clear that the goal is the same

### 2. Design
- System structure and schedule pressure collide
- Yeonjin proposes removing promises that cannot be kept first
- Donggun makes his first decision to reflect marketing judgment in technical design

### 3. Development
- All-night work and repeated failures
- Emotional clashes occur within the team
- Han Doyun's humor buffers the atmosphere
- Yeonjin organizes the team with the perspective of a leader, not emotions

### 4. Testing
- **Compensation logic errors** and **signs of payment abnormalities** are discovered simultaneously during QA
  - Compensation: Probability is biased towards one side under certain conditions, undermining fairness
  - Payment: The possibility of duplicate payments is confirmed in the retry flow
- Internal team conflict: Arguments for stopping payments first vs. upholding compensation fairness first
- Yeonjin proposes **standards to prevent trust from collapsing** before technical solutions
  - Payments will be blocked immediately if a duplicate possibility is detected
  - Compensation will be delayed if fairness standards are not set and the line is crossed
- Donggun convinces Chanwoo to handle both risks simultaneously
- Decision to postpone the schedule

### 5. Service Launch
- Quiet but stable launch success
- A sense of relief is formed throughout the team
- Private feelings naturally begin to arise between Donggun and Yeonjin

---

## Act 2 ─ Two People Who Fit Too Well (Time of Stability)

### 6. Quiet Relationship
- The relationship continues without a declaration of love
- The rhythm of work and life naturally aligns
- Attitudes towards problems and value judgments are similar

### 7. Accumulation of Daily Life
- Time passes without any special events
- The reader feels that this relationship could last a long time

※ No omens of anxiety, cracks, or departures exist in this section

---

## Act 3 ─ Choices Without Explanation

### 8. External Shock
- Sudden death of Yeonjin's father
- Only the result is presented without direct emotional depiction
- Immediately after the funeral, notification of the will from a lawyer

### 9. Yeonjin's Perspective
- Minimal disclosure of the Planthub past narrative
- Father bequeaths 30% of Planthub shares to Yeonjin
- Condition: **Automatic transfer to Chae Dongsu if not returned within 3 months**
- Recollection of value clashes with Chae Dongsu
- Chae Dongsu's pressure: "Your father gave you the last choice. Return within 3 months, or give up forever."
- Yeonjin is placed in a situation where she can no longer choose
- If she gives up the shares, her father's will disappears, and if she returns, she must leave XPlan

### 10. Breakup and Resignation
- Yeonjin resigns from XPlan citing studying abroad as the reason
- Ends her relationship with Donggun
- Breaks up without explanation, apology, or promise
- Donggun asks for the reason, but Yeonjin only replies, "I can't tell you right now"

---

## Act 4 ─ The Collapse of the One Left Behind

### 11. Donggun's Descent
- Daily life collapses
- Chanwoo witnesses Donggun's condition
- Chanwoo: "I need you, but you need to rest now. I'll protect the company."
- Han Doyun witnesses Donggun's daily life collapsing up close
- Han Doyun tries to buffer the situation with humor, but fails
- Han Doyun books a flight to Thailand on his behalf
- Resigns from the company
- Leaves for Thailand

### 12. Thailand Episode (Lin)
- Reunited with Lin, an early XPlan member
- Concretization of Lin's past reason for leaving: "I didn't hate the structure you created. I couldn't stand the way you postponed choices within it."
- Presentation of a way of life that chose freedom
- Lin: "You've always failed to hold onto people who leave. Because you didn't choose first."
- Donggun recognizes his repeated choice pattern
- Realization that Lin and Yeonjin left for the same reason

---

## Act 5 ─ The Structure Moves

### 13. Internal Cracks
- Signs of abnormalities within XPlan during Donggun's absence
- **New character appears: Kang Minseok (XPlan CTO)**
- Kang Minseok discovers abnormalities: Planthub releases features similar to XPlan first
- Donggun's absence → Code review process collapses → Security vulnerabilities exposed
- Seeds of suspicion of technology leakage arise
- Han Doyun senses the anxious atmosphere within the team
- Han Doyun: "The team members are anxious. Someone needs to make a decision."

### 14. Chanwoo's Judgment
- Chanwoo receives Kang Minseok's report
- Chanwoo recognizes the problem as a structural issue, not individual betrayal
- Organizes the internal situation with Han Doyun
- Begins searching for external alternatives
- **XPlan's initial investor, Lee Junho** advises Chanwoo
- Lee Junho: "In this situation, meet Jung Haewon. He's someone who designs structures."

### 15. The Designer's Appearance
- Chanwoo contacts Jung Haewon through Lee Junho's introduction
- Discussion of designing a structure to block a hostile takeover
- Jung Haewon: "There is a way to survive without selling the company."

---

## Act 6 ─ Yeonjin's Return Path

### 16. Yeonjin in the Study Abroad Location
- Quiet life and distancing
- Emotionally disconnected from Planthub
- The 3-month deadline is approaching

### 17. Yoon Sera's Visit
- Yoon Sera is skeptical of Chae Dongsu's aggressive M&A approach
- Not persuasion, but notification of reality
- Only presents numbers, structure, and results
- Yoon Sera: "Chae Dongsu is trying to take over XPlan hostilely. You're the only one who can put a brake on it from the inside."
- Leaves the choice to Yeonjin

### 18. Yeonjin's Decision
- Not returning, but **'choosing to prevent Chae Dongsu's hostile takeover of XPlan'**
- Presents return conditions:
  - Planthub subsidiary director title
  - Granting of acquisition condition negotiation rights
- Yeonjin's intention: To design **favorable conditions** for XPlan as a Planthub director
- Decision to join Planthub

---

## Act 7 ─ Reappearance and Conflict

### 19. Merger Situation
- Planthub's proposal for an XPlan merger and acquisition is formalized
- Internal division of XPlan intensifies
  - **Proponents**: Finance/Management (priority on survival)
  - **Opponents**: Technology/Planning Team (priority on independence)
- Han Doyun concretizes the internal division
- Donggun urgently returns from Thailand at Chanwoo's request

### 20. Yeonjin's Reappearance
- First negotiation table held
- **Planthub side**: Chae Dongsu (CEO), Yoon Sera (Head of Strategic Planning), Yeonjin (Subsidiary Director), Park Jaehyun (CFO, new character)
- **XPlan side**: Chanwoo (CEO), Donggun (Technical Director), Han Doyun (PM), Jung Haewon (External Investor)
- Yeonjin appears as Planthub's chief negotiator
- First re-encounter with Donggun
- Only tension exists without explanation
- At the negotiation table, Yeonjin begins to propose conditions favorable to XPlan

---

## Act 8 ─ Reconstruction of Truth

### 21. Truth Revealed
- Clarification of the reality of information leakage
- Yeonjin's position and choices are reinterpreted

### 22. Redefinition of Relationship
- Short conversation between Donggun and Yeonjin
- No excuses or apologies

---

## Act 9 ─ Same Direction

### 23. Each One's Choice
- Company direction organized
- Donggun and Yeonjin complete their choices in their respective positions

### 24. Ending
- Each chooses to go to Canada
- Brief encounter at the airport departure hall
- Whether or not they reunite is not specified
