## 🔗 Character Relationship Analysis Summary

### **Core Relationships**
- **Donggeon ↔ Yeonjin**: Trust-based partnership → Unexplained separation → Reunion at the negotiation table. The theme is "Choice/Responsibility."
- **Donggeon ↔ Chanwoo**: Co-founders and complementary figures. Chanwoo structurally fills Donggeon's absence.
- **Yeonjin ↔ Chaedongsu**: Family/values conflict axis. Family relationships strengthen the justification and tragic nature of the conflict.
- **Yeonjin ↔ Yunsera**: Alliance based on "reality notification" rather than persuasion. Provides justification for Yeonjin's return.
- **Chanwoo ↔ Jeong Haewon**: Structural design partner who "saves instead of selling."

### **Supporting Relationships**
- **Donggeon ↔ Rin**: A mirror reflecting the current pattern of past departures.
- **Chanwoo ↔ Lee Junho**: A bridge/mentor leading to solutions.
- **Han Doyoon**: Internal emotional buffer + observer of division (reader's viewpoint).
- **Kang Minseok/Park Jaehyeon**: Functional figures who drive technical crises/financial negotiations.

## ⚠️ Weak Coherence Areas

### 1. **Act 3 → Act 4 Transition (Yeonjin's Choice)**

**Current State:**
```
8. External Shock
- Yeonjin's father's sudden death
- Only the result is presented without direct emotional depiction

9. Yeonjin's Perspective
- Minimal disclosure of PlantHub's past narrative
- Clash of values with Chaedongsu
- Yeonjin is placed in a situation where she can no longer choose

10. Farewell and Resignation
- Yeonjin resigns from XPlan under the pretense of studying abroad
- Relationship with Donggeon ends
- Separation without explanation, apology, or promises
```

**Problem:**
- ❌ **Lack of causal link** between "Father's sudden death → Pressure to return to PlantHub"
- ❌ Why does Yeonjin have to return when her father dies?
- ❌ Chaedongsu's methods of pressure are not specific
- ❌ Why Yeonjin is "unable to choose" is unclear to the reader

**Improvement Needs:**
1. Specify father's will/inheritance conditions
2. Explain PlantHub's ownership structure
3. Need specific scenes of Chaedongsu's pressure
4. Present **legal/economic reasons** Yeonjin cannot refuse

**Suggestion:**
- Father bequeaths 30% of PlantHub shares to Yeonjin
- However, a condition that if she does not return within 3 months, the shares are automatically transferred to Chaedongsu
- Or: Yeonjin co-guaranteed her father's debt during his lifetime
- Chaedongsu demands her return as a condition for debt repayment

### 2. **Act 5 13-14 (Internal Division in XPlan)**

**Current State:**
```
13. Internal Division
- Signs of internal problems at XPlan during Donggeon's absence
- Seed of suspicion of technology leakage

14. Chanwoo's Judgment
- Chanwoo recognizes the problem as structural rather than individual
- Begins exploring external alternatives
```

**Problem:**
- ❌ It is unclear **who** caused the "technology leakage suspicion" and **how** it occurred
- ❌ **Weak causal relationship** between Donggeon's absence and technology leakage
- ❌ Lack of basis for Chanwoo to "recognize it as structural"
- ❌ No one the reader can suspect at this point

**Improvement Needs:**
1. **Specific origin** of the technology leakage suspicion (e.g., competing company releases similar function)
2. Need for internal whistleblower or audit personnel
3. **Evidence collection process** for Chanwoo to make a judgment
4. Specify **security gaps** due to Donggeon's absence

**Suggestion:**
- Add new character: **XPlan CTO or Security Officer**
- This person reports technology leakage suspicion to Chanwoo
- Donggeon's absence → Code review process collapses → Security vulnerabilities exposed
- PlantHub releases a function similar to XPlan first

---

### 3. **Act 6 17-18 (Yunsera's Intervention)**

**Current State:**
```
17. Yunsera's Visit
- Reality notification rather than persuasion
- Presents only numbers, structure, and results
- Leaves the choice to Yeonjin

18. Yeonjin's Decision
- A choice to 'go to stop' rather than return
- Proposes return conditions
- Decides to join PlantHub
```

**Problem:**
- ❌ **Why does Yunsera visit Yeonjin?** (On Chaedongsu's orders? Voluntarily?)
- ❌ It is unclear **what** Yeonjin is "going to stop"
- ❌ The reader does not know what "return conditions" Yeonjin proposes
- ❌ Joining PlantHub ≠ Stopping XPlan (logical leap)

**Improvement Needs:**
1. Clarify Yunsera's motivation
2. What Yeonjin is trying to stop: **Chaedongsu's hostile takeover of XPlan**
3. Specify Yeonjin's return conditions (e.g., independent authority of the Strategic Planning Office)
4. **Structural basis** for Yeonjin to stop it internally

**Suggestion:**
- Yunsera is skeptical of Chaedongsu's aggressive M&A methods
- Asks Yeonjin to "put a brake on it internally"
- Yeonjin's condition: Subsidiary director title + Negotiation rights for acquisition conditions
- Yeonjin intends to design **favorable conditions** for XPlan as a PlantHub director

---

### 4. **Act 7 19-20 (Reappearance and Conflict)**

**Current State:**
```
19. Merger Phase
- PlantHub's proposal to acquire XPlan is formalized
- Internal division within XPlan intensifies

20. Yeonjin's Reappearance
- Appears as a director of PlantHub's subsidiary
- First reunion with Donggeon
- Only tension exists without explanation
```

**Problem:**
- ❌ **Lack of specific aspects** of "internal division within XPlan"
- ❌ Who approves the merger? Who opposes it?
- ❌ Donggeon's location is unclear (Did he return from Thailand?)
- ❌ It is ambiguous **in what situation** the reunion scene takes place

**Improvement Needs:**
1. Specify XPlan internal faction
   - Approval faction: Financial/management (survival priority)
   - Opposition faction: Technology/planning team (independence priority)
2. Specify Donggeon's return timing
3. Set the **physical space** for the reunion scene (Meeting room? Negotiation table?)

**Suggestion:**
- Specify internal division through Han Doyoon
- Donggeon urgently returns from Thailand at Chanwoo's request
- Reunion occurs at the **first negotiation table**
- Yeonjin appears as the PlantHub's negotiation representative

## 👥 Areas Lacking Character Placement

### **Serious Gap: Act 4 (Donggeon's Collapse)**

**Current State:**
```
11. Donggeon's Descent
- Collapse of daily life
- Steps down from the company
- Leaves for Thailand

12. Thailand Episode (Rin)
- Reunited with Rin, an early member of XPlan
- Presents a way of life that chose freedom
- Donggeon recognizes his repeated selection pattern
```

**Problem:**
- ❌ **Lack of a character witnessing** Donggeon's collapse
- ❌ No reaction from colleagues such as Chanwoo and Han Doyoon
- ❌ **No character intervening** in Donggeon's process of leaving for Thailand
- ❌ No character talking to Donggeon in Thailand other than Rin

**Necessary Character Placement:**

1. **Chanwoo (Add to Scene 11)**
   - Witnesses Donggeon's collapse and recommends leave of absence
   - "I need you, but you need to rest now"
   - Promise to protect the company

2. **Han Doyoon (Add to Scene 11)**
   - Witnesses Donggeon's collapse of daily life most closely
   - Tries to buffer with humor but fails
   - Plays the role of booking a flight ticket to Thailand on his behalf

3. **Rin (Strengthen Scene 12)**
   - Currently a one-time appearance, but **lack of conversation density**
   - Present Rin's past departure reasons more specifically
   - Delivers the core insight to Donggeon: "You always postponed your choices"
