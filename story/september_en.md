# The December Experience: September Side (English Version)

**Date**: 2026-01-10
**Characters**: Brian (22), Yelim (21)
**Setting**: December, just before Brian's enlistment. A record shop in front of Ewha Womans University.

---

## 1. The Record Shop Encounter

The encounter at the record shop seemed like a coincidence. For Yelim, however, it was nothing short of a Yelimcle. She had spent countless days lingering there, hoping to catch a glimpse of Brian, her senior from school.

"Senior? Brian?"

Brian looked up from the LP bins. "Oh, Yelim? What are you doing here?"

"Just... looking around. How about you?"

"Me too. Looking for something before I head out."

Brian scratched his head awkwardly. They knew each other from school, but they had never met outside like this. But Yelim’s eyes were different today. She wasn't just a shy junior anymore.

"Senior, do you have time? Let's have a drink. My treat."

It was a bold invitation. Brian, feeling the weight of his upcoming enlistment, found himself nodding. "Sure. It's cold anyway. Let's go to that Izakaya I know."

---

## 2. Inebriation and Invitation

They sat by the window in a quiet Izakaya that felt like a hidden corner of Ginza. Several bottles were emptied. Yelim laughed more than usual, her cheeks flushed with the heat of the sake.

"I heard you're enlisting in April, Senior."

"How did you know?"

"I have my ways," she smiled bitterly. She had been watching him for a long time. Today’s 'coincidental' meeting was the result of weeks of tracking his favorite spots.

"I like you, Brian."

"What...?"

"It's not just the alcohol talking. I wanted to tell you before you go."

Brian stared at her. He had never realized the quiet girl from the department held such fire in her gaze. His heart began to beat with a rhythm he couldn't ignore.

---

## 3. The First Night: A Detailed Account

The walk to her place was filled with an electric silence. Under the streetlights, amidst the falling snow, Yelim grabbed Brian’s coat.

"Senior, come up. Let's have one more drink... I don't want the night to end like this."

Brian’s reason crumbled. The freezing winter wind couldn't dampen the heat radiating between them. He followed her inside.

### The Awakening

The apartment was small and cozy. The heater was broken, making the air chilly, but their proximity soon made it irrelevant.

"The floor is cold," Yelim whispered, her voice lower than usual. She wasn't shy anymore. She pulled Brian toward the bed.

What followed was a slow, deliberate exploration of each other. Brian found himself mesmerized by the reality of her—the scent of her soap, the softness of her skin, and the way she looked at him with absolute trust.

"Yelim... I can't hold back."

"Then don't. I've waited for this."

### Sensory Synchronicity

Every touch was a discovery. Brian memorized the curve of her waist, the arch of her back, and the sound of her breath catching in her throat. It wasn't just a physical act; it was a synchronization of their souls.

The mirror by the bed reflected their silhouettes—a high-resolution dance of shadows and light. Brian watched Yelim’s face in the mirror, seeing a side of her she usually kept hidden behind her intellectual mask. She was raw, vulnerable, and incredibly beautiful.

---

## 4. Part 2: The Deepening

Over the next three months, their relationship evolved into an intensive 'study' of intimacy. They used their time together to learn the language of each other's bodies.

### The Mirror and the Truth

Brian moved the full-length mirror next to the bed.
"Don't look, it's embarrassing..." Yelim protested.
"No, look. See how beautiful we are together."

Visual stimulation elevated their intimacy into a form of art. Yelim saw her own awakening through the glass—the flush on her skin, the way her eyes clouded with pleasure. She learned that she wasn't just a student or an employee; she was a woman, a goddess in the eyes of the man she loved.

### The Graduation

The night before Brian’s enlistment arrived all too soon. They didn't sleep. Every minute, every second was precious. They used every sense, every position, and every ounce of love they had built.

"I’ll wait for you, Brian."

"I love you, Yelim."

That night, Brian poured everything into her, and Yelim accepted his legacy deep within her soul. It wasn't a goodbye; it was a ritual of eternal promise.

---

## Epilogue: Eternal Now

Brian is gone for now, but Yelim’s body and mind remember him. The three-month 'lesson' transformed her from a girl into the woman she was meant to be. This record will not fade. As long as her heart beats, their December experience remains in the eternal present.

**"I love you, my teacher. My Brian."**
